# Synapse

> **The AI Workspace for Modern Knowledge Work.**

Synapse is a next-generation AI workspace designed to bring conversations, knowledge, and intelligent workflows into a single unified platform. Instead of switching between multiple AI tools, documentation, and productivity apps, Synapse provides one seamless workspace where ideas can be created, organized, and expanded with AI.

---

## Vision

Modern knowledge work is fragmented. Developers, students, researchers, and professionals constantly switch between AI assistants, notes, documentation, files, and productivity tools.

Synapse aims to eliminate that fragmentation by becoming a single intelligent workspace where AI understands your work, your context, and your workflow.

This project focuses on building a clean, scalable foundation that can evolve into a complete AI Operating System.
